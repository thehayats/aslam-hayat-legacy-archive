# The Value of Time

*In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.*

Time is a capital that, once spent, can never be recovered. Allah Almighty swore by time in the Holy Quran — *"Wal-'Asr"* — and from this alone we may gauge its worth and significance in the divine reckoning.

## Time and Human Life

Human life is, in essence, the guest of time. Each of us has been granted a defined duration, and once that duration has passed, no means remain to extend it. To waste the moments we possess is therefore to waste life itself.

Our elders held this maxim:

> *"Time is like a sword — if you do not cut it, it will cut you."*

The image is stark but true: time does not wait, and its passing is not neutral. Each hour spent well is a brick laid in the edifice of a meaningful life; each hour squandered is mortar that hardens into regret.

## Scholars and the Reverence for Time

Among all the great scholars and accomplished persons of history, one common quality stands out: they regarded time as extraordinarily precious. It is well known that Imam Bukhari, may Allah have mercy upon him, would rise from his bed many times in a single night, light a lamp, and record whatever insight had come to him in that moment — lest it be lost by morning.

In Allama Iqbal's thought too, this sense of time runs very deep. He urged Muslims repeatedly to awaken to the perishability of the moment and the urgency of the present hour, drawing on both Islamic tradition and his own philosophical vision.

## A Message for the Young

To the young people of today I wish to say one thing clearly: your youth is your greatest blessing. The energy, the intelligence, the health that you possess right now will not remain forever. Use this time to learn, to grow, and to build something of lasting worth.

Hours lost to mindless scrolling through social media, time frittered away in purposeless conversation — these represent a kind of injustice toward one's own future self.

## A Prayer

May Allah grant us all the understanding to recognise the value of time and the ability to employ it in righteous purpose. Ameen.
