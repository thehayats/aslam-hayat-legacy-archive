# Welcome to The Aslam Hayat Legacy Archive

*بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ*

We are honoured to launch this digital archive as a permanent home for the life, writings, and published work of **Aslam Hayat** — scholar, writer, devoted father, and beloved member of our community.

## Why This Archive

Aslam Hayat wrote over 650 articles in Urdu across more than twenty-five years. He kept a library. He stayed in contact with family and readers through Facebook into his final years. This archive preserves that record — his writing, his photographs, and the recollections of those who knew him — in a form that will outlast the platforms it was originally published on.

## What You Will Find Here

**The Articles** section contains his Urdu writings alongside English translations, so that readers of both languages can access his thought. The side-by-side translation feature allows the two languages to be read together — the original Urdu on the right, the English rendering on the left, separated by a thin gold line, as if in conversation.

**The Timeline** traces his life from his birth in Lahore in 1933 through the decades of his professional work, his family life, and his final years of continued writing and reflection.

**The Gallery** collects photographs from across his life — professional occasions, family gatherings, moments of quiet domesticity, and public recognition.

**The Facebook Archive** preserves the posts and reflections he shared in the last years of his life, when social media gave him a new and unexpectedly wide audience for his daily meditations.

## An Invitation

This archive is not a finished project. It is a living document, and we need your help to make it complete.

If you knew Aslam Hayat — as a colleague, a student, a reader, a neighbour, or a friend — we invite you to submit your memories. A brief anecdote, a photograph, a letter he wrote to you, a memory of something he said that stayed with you — all of these matter and all of them will be welcomed.

Please visit the **[Submit a Memory](/contact)** page to share what you hold.
